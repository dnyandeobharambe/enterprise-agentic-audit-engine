=====================================================
INFRASTRUCTURE COMPLIANCE POLICY - v2026.04
=====================================================

1. HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS
------------------------
- All active nodes must have a minimum of 16GB RAM.
- Nodes with 8GB or less are considered "CRITICAL NON-COMPLIANCE."

2. SOFTWARE & FIRMWARE
----------------------
- The minimum allowed firmware version is 4.2.
- Any node running firmware 4.0 or lower must be flagged for immediate update.

3. SITE REDUNDANCY
------------------
- Every site must maintain at least 2 active nodes to ensure high availability.
- Single-node sites are a violation of the redundancy policy.

4. MAINTENANCE STATUS
---------------------
- Nodes in 'Maintenance' status are exempt from RAM requirements but must still meet firmware standards before being returned to 'Active' status.

[END OF POLICY]
